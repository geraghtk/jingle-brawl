Full rules

Grump's Gambit

  • Triggers when nobody bids on your opened gift
  • Keep the gift OR challenge anyone!
  • Pay Minimum Cost to Bank

"Why settle when you can steal..."

Reindeer Reprisal

  • Triggers when you LOSE a sealed-bid duel
  • Challenge any gift (except the one you just lost and your own)
  • None after a Gambit, yield, tie-break or endgame duel
  • No chaining: one Reprisal per duel
  • Pick a Misfit? Claim it, no duel
  • Pay Minimum Cost to Bank

"Because reindeer kick back..."

Minimum Cost

  • For Grump's Gambit, Reprisals & Steals
  • Pay to the Bank (no pot created)
  • Defending is always FREE
Cost = 1 + Naughty Level

Swap Algorithm

  • Winner takes contested gift
  • Winner's old gift → Loser
  • If Loser had a gift → their original goes to Misfit
  • Gift to Misfit resets to Naughty 0
  • Player who loses gift to Misfit → new Head Elf (table play)

Santa Tax

  • Applies to sealed bid pots of 2+ chips
  • Winner takes at most 2 chips
  • Rest → Bank (tax)

Loser's Dividend

  • Lose a duel as the Defender? Get +1 chip from Bank
  • Attackers who lose get nothing
  • Exception: No dividend for misfit, yield, or tie-break duels

Yield Duels

  • Opener can yield if there's a 2nd bidder
  • Duel becomes Challenger 1 vs Challenger 2
  • Both pay half their bid (rounded down)
  • Opener's name goes back in Draw Bag

Party Tiebreakers

  • Sketch-Off or Whack-an-Elf: everyone plays
  • Best-placed tied bidder wins the tie
  • Top 3 score 3 / 2 / 1 party points
  • Best non-tied finisher: +1 chip Elf Bonus
  • Party Score = party points + duels fought + correct duel calls → Party MVP and the final standings (leftover chips count for nothing)

Picking the Duel

  • The Defender picks from the host's list (or spins)
  • Yield & tiebreak duels: the wheel picks
  • Under 16? You always pick (youngest wins a clash). Tick it when joining
  • An in-app game (Whack-an-Elf, AI Draw Off, Pass the Gremlin, Ring Toss, Gift Conveyor, Nutcracker Knockout, Mecha Santa Mayhem, Elf Tower Wars…) settles it: better-placed duellist wins
  • With 4+ players the team games are on the list by default: the duellists captain, everyone plays
  • Spectators call it once the duel is known; the host waits for the votes

Games on the Phones

  • Playing along while two others duel earns party points: Pass the Gremlin 1 if it never catches you, Ring Toss 3 / 2 / 1 by your own score; in a team game (Gift Conveyor, Mecha Santa, Elf Tower Wars) everyone on the winning team gets 1
  • Before a phone game: read how to play, press Ready; it starts when everyone has (3, 2, 1)
  • First time a phone game comes up: a short practice round that does not count, then the same game for real

Endgame (Misfit Lottery)

  • Giftless players enter one by one
  • Draw oldest Misfit (FIFO)
  • Path A: Steal from any player (pay cost)
  • Path B: Auction the Misfit
  • No Loser's Dividend in endgame
  • When the night is over: the host can press Play again with the same people: everyone keeps their seat, gifts, chips and scores start again
