Columns: focal seat profit per game by habit; "pair" = joint profit of two seats, one always backing A, one always B (collusion probe);
net = chips the betting adds to (+) or removes from (-) the whole table per game (table of the row habit, no deviant); tax = what the duel tax burned in those games;
pool vs p: mean |closing pool share - true p| (line = the record line alone, half = always 50/50), slope of pool share on p (1 = calibrated), two-sided = duels with real backers on both sides.
